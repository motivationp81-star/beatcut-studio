# BeatCut Studio

**v2 — rebuilt CapCut-style:** big preview up top, one-tap **Auto Edit**, a visual timeline with beat ticks, song trim sliders, cut-speed control, hook text, and MP4 export where the browser supports it.

Edit video **on the beat of your song** — right in your browser. Add your clips and an audio file, and BeatCut listens to the song, finds its beats, and cuts, shakes and flashes exactly on them. No uploads, no server, no editing timeline to learn.

**▶ Try it live:** https://motivationp81-star.github.io/beatcut-studio/

## Modes

- **Clips + Song** — drop in one or more video clips and a song; get an auto beat-cut montage.
- **Song Only** — give just a song (and an optional photo) and get a beat-reactive visualizer video with your lyrics on it.
- **Photos + Song** — your photos become a collage edit that changes on the beat, with sparkle and heart stickers.

## Style presets

- **Funk Dhamaaka** — fast cuts on every beat, glitch slices and shake on the drop, zoom punches.
- **Lyrics King** — slower cuts so the words can breathe; built for lyric videos.
- **Dreamy Romantic** — slow zooms, soft colour, hearts and flowers floating on strong beats.
- **Cinematic** — heavy grade, slow push-ins, trailer feel.

## Text styles

Type your lyric lines (Hinglish works fine) and pick how they appear:

- **Word by word — big pop** with a yellow-orange gradient
- **Word cards — red on black**, one word at a time between shots
- **Word by word — rotate in**, Alight-Motion style
- **Line by line — soft fade** serif-style captions

## Also inside

- A real **beat engine**: low-frequency energy analysis with adaptive onset detection — slow songs get calm edits, fast funk gets fast cuts, automatically.
- **Effects**: glitch on the drop, beat shake, white flash, zoom punch, stickers, vignette.
- **Quality pack**: colour clean + contrast, sharpening, and 720p or 1080p export.
- **Export** renders in real time and downloads a `.webm` video with the song mixed in — ready for Instagram or YouTube.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with the Web Audio API, Canvas and MediaRecorder. Everything runs locally on your device; your files are never uploaded anywhere.
